Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Do I need to pay to use JibikaLinks?

No. The three free interfaces are accessible without purchasing Sirat Search services.

Are the search results verified vacancies?

No. Google indexes publicly accessible pages and posts; results may include older or closed jobs. Verify each opportunity at the original source.

Do you guarantee a job offer?

No. The services are designed to improve the search process; hiring decisions depend on employers and individual circumstances.

Can I buy a digital workbook immediately?

Contact me to check availability and scope. There is currently no automatic checkout or payment-based download on this site.

Privacy & analytics

Your choice matters.
